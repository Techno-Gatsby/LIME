Compare documents pairs changed paragraphs the way LibreOffice Writer's Compare Document does (sw/source/core/doc/doccomp.cxx,
MPL-2.0, The Document Foundation): lengths within three times each other, and enough text in long common stretches
(the sum of their squared lengths).
The wheel's liquid glass follows liquid-glass-react by rdev (https://github.com/rdev/liquid-glass-react, MIT License; its
displacement generator is adapted from shuding/liquid-glass): a displacement map drawn on a canvas and fed through feImage to
feDisplacementMap, the red, green and blue channels each displaced a different distance and screen-blended, and the elastic
lean toward the pointer. The bevel-zone refraction, the chromatic aberration that grows with it (blue furthest), the specular
light that moves on its own and the viscous follow of the pointer are after liquidGL by naughtyduk
(https://github.com/naughtyduk/liquidGL, MIT License). No code is copied from either.
